Journey view · the decision trail

Delivery → revision → new delivery → acceptance

One trail, newest last. Each decision binds the exact delivery revision and criteria version; revisions produce a new version and cannot inherit a stale approval.

Journey view · refused states

What refusal looks like

The failure paths are drawn states, so they can be reviewed before they exist in code. Color is paired with words; every refusal names the rule it enforces.

Approval for rev 1refused · stale version

Rev 2 exists; this approval bound rev 1. Stale-version rejection: a superseded delivery cannot hold a live approval.

Approval by @kofirefused · role

@kofi authored the delivery and holds no QA role in this milestone. No self-granted reviewer authority.

Duplicate decisionrejected

@sana's scope+evidence decision on rev 2 is already recorded; a second, identical decision adds no threshold unit.

Absent reviewerwaiting

QA slot empty past the escalation deadline → the named dispute path is opened automatically. Unavailable decision-makers are a designed state, not an exception.

Criteria addressed
Release criteria: stale-version and duplicate decision rejection · authorization checked at the decision point · conflicting approvals, absent reviewers, role revocation and disputed evidence exercised · no self-granted reviewer authority.
