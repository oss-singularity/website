Journey view · offers versus commitments

Offer and commitment are different states

An offer is one participant's input. A commitment binds two or more participants to a scope version, review responsibility and change/exit terms. The visual difference is deliberate: outline card for the offer, accent border and bound badges for the commitment.

Offerno agreement yet

"I can build the digest verifier in the first half of October." Posted by @kofi toward Verification toolkit · scope v2. Nothing is agreed; posting an offer assigns no task and grants no role.

Criteria addressed
Offers and needs recorded separately from actual commitments · publishing a card does not assign a task.
