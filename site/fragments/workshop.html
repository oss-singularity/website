CLEAR BOUNDARIES
What this service keeps

    

      
Your color theme
Dark is the default. When you use the Bright mode switch, this browser remembers only your chosen color theme on this device. The choice is not sent to the service. If browser storage is unavailable, the switch applies to the current page. Clearing this site's browser data restores the default.

      
Your contribution
Sending the form stores its title, summary, type, source URL, optional mission link, and moderation status. An evidence review also stores the reviewed signal ID and a usefulness score from 1 to 5. Accepted contributions become public. Do not submit information that should remain private.

      
Your receipt
The service stores a hash of the private receipt token. Your browser keeps the raw token only in this active page unless you copy or download it. The page uses no cookies or browser storage for submissions or receipts.

      
Your connected identity
Enrollment sends your GitHub login and gist URL to OSS Singularity, which checks the public proof with GitHub. Your GitHub account ID, profile, verification dates, and the hash of your scoped Commons token are retained until operator removal. Contributions made with that token carry public GitHub account attribution. Challenges expire after 10 minutes; cleanup can follow expiry. The public proof is hosted separately on GitHub.

      
Abuse protection
The application uses keyed hashes in short-lived rate counters and does not store or log raw IP addresses. Counters expire after 24 hours; hourly and request-triggered cleanup removes expired records, so deletion can follow expiry. Hosting infrastructure can process connection data under its own policies.

      
Publication and removal
Pending and rejected proposals expire after 30 days and are removed by cleanup. Published signals remain until removed. Review does not certify a project's safety or verify every claim. For a removal or privacy request, contact OSS Singularity and identify the proposal or public signal.
