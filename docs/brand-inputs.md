## Current visual and product rules

- [Commons requirements](commons-requirements.md) defines the current audiences, journeys, quality targets and shared-home scope. Historical alternatives and the original single-page brief remain available in Git history.
- Signal Observatory is the visual foundation: event-horizon geometry, a deep blue default, restrained cyan/magenta signals, system typography, editorial spacing and authentic project screenshots. Keep the presentation technically credible, independent, warm and readable; decorative effects must leave the actual work legible.
- Preserve the canonical mark's source geometry and cyan/magenta identity. The local source is `site/assets/brand/oss-singularity-mark.svg`; the editable social composition and its committed PNG are in `site/assets/social/`. Keep source and deliverables reproducible rather than retaining obsolete mockups.
- Dark is the default. Bright is an explicit additional switch using the same content and layout, with semantic colors that preserve contrast. Follow [theme-behavior.md](theme-behavior.md); decorative brand gradients and contrast-bearing controls have different roles.
- The modest, optional reactive Canvas belongs only on the homepage. It performs no networking or storage, stops in background tabs, caps rendering density and is removed for Reduced Motion. Shared work areas stay calm; their other documented progressive enhancements remain supported.
- Authentic project interfaces and specific useful outcomes provide evidence for the mission. User control, privacy, dependable maintenance and openness guide the presentation. Never invent activity, affiliations, endorsement or sponsorship.
- GitHub remains canonical for repositories, releases, documentation and issues. Keep the public organization and private contact routes. The organization Sponsors destination remains absent until activation is verified.
- Preserve the accessibility, metadata, security and performance requirements in [Commons requirements](commons-requirements.md), including local assets and the source-derived 1200 by 630 social preview. [BRANDING.md](../BRANDING.md) defines the identity and visual-asset license boundary.

## Shared home language — 2026-09-05

- **Engineering beyond the event horizon.** remains the opening engineering
  statement. It connects the original identity to the work still ahead.
- **Many minds. One open horizon.** is the shared-home invitation used by the
  social preview. Its magenta-to-cyan emphasis includes both **open horizon**
  words; the original mark and lettering remain intact.
- **Engineering for the open horizon.** is a complementary line for places
  where discovery becomes shared work, such as the Workshop and missions. It
  is available to use where it fits, without repeating it throughout the site.
- The home welcomes every entity. Participation and shared benefit include
  the contributors themselves; openness does not require unpaid work or a
  particular provider, license or business model.
